WHAT THIS FILE IS
------------------
The YKI-valmennus section (9 chapters, one per official YKI theme) is
transcribed from a study-guide/workbook source, chapter by chapter, as the
source material was pasted in. Chapters 1-6 arrived essentially complete
(full reading articles, full listening scripts, individually scripted
writing/speaking test tasks). Chapters 7-9 arrived with real gaps in the
source itself — most visibly a reading article that was never extracted
into the text, and several task sections reduced to generic topic-name
lists instead of the book's usual specific numbered exercises with answer
keys.

Per CLAUDE.md's rule against fabricating content, every gap below was
represented honestly in the app (a visible "not available" note, or a
category-summary list of topics instead of invented full scenarios)
rather than filled in with made-up material. This file is the checklist
for going back and closing those gaps once real source content (the
missing pages/scans/audio) is available.

Two constants in ykiPrep.ts mark the honest placeholders in the data:
  NO_ARTICLE_NOTE    — reading passage's source article text was never
                       extracted from the book.
  NO_TRANSCRIPT_NOTE — listening passage has no printed script/audio in
                       the source material we received.
